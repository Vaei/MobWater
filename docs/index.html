MobWater

Water for mobile forward rendering path. Ponds, lakes, pools and ocean, that a character wades into and swims in. Ripples, wakes and splashes from anything that moves through it. And waves a dedicated server evaluates to the same answer the client draws.

Install it →

Not officially released. Expect bugs and updates without versioning. Documentation is still filling in.

Why

Water that shallows at the bank, foams where it meets the shore, and shows the bed through it. Waves that lie down as they reach the shore instead of cutting through it. Ripples that spread from anything moving in the water and reflect off whatever is standing in it.

Boats sit at the right height on the client and the server both.

All of it costs one translucent plane, and runs anywhere - including the mobile forward path, where most water does not.

What you get

Four shapesBox and disc for the pools a level actually has in it, a spline that is a lake closed and a river open, and an ocean that follows the view. Selecting one compiles only its own edge arithmetic.
Water knows how deep it isThe bed comes from the depth buffer, so the column's thickness is a tap already being paid for. Colour, clarity, shoreline foam and the underwater fade all read off that one number.
Waves lie down at the bankAmplitude is weighted by distance to the body's own edge, so displacement dies at the shore instead of clipping through it. That one term is what stops a lake's rim shimmering above dry ground.
Bodies are dataColour, foam, glint and the rest live in custom primitive data, so a jade temple pool and a grey harbour are the same material and share every permutation. A look preset is a copy, not a link.
Waterfalls that join at both endsA sheet drawn by how long ago the water left the lip, so the pattern rides down with it and stretches as it accelerates. The top follows the river feeding it and the base churns the pool below. Its own material, because nothing a body of water means by its geometry survives being stood on end.
Ripples, wakes and splashesA real wave equation on one camera-following field, so ripples spread and reflect off whatever is standing in them. A second body reading the field is not a second cost.
An ocean that is a spectrumA Phillips sea solved offline and sampled rather than summed, because a handful of sines can only ever look like a handful of sines. It loops exactly, and only a body that asks for one carries it.
A surface a server agrees withThe waves are a pure function of position and time. Buoyancy on the client and buoyancy on the server are the same arithmetic, and Verify Contract asserts the CPU and the GPU still agree - and the boats float on it.
Nothing off is paid forRipples, foam, foam texture and refraction are separate shaders rather than amounts of zero, because the values arrive as per-instance data the compiler cannot fold.

Ten minutes to a pond

  1. Clone, build, enable, then Water → Set Up Water.
  2. Drag a Water Pool out of the Water menu into a hollow.
  3. Set Extent and Depth. Do not scale the actor.
  4. Pick a Look Preset and press Apply Look Preset.
  5. Walk into it.

Light reading

Mobile Forward Rendering guidewhy this path exists and how to get on it. Comes with a starter project
MobMaterialsthe masters for the same renderer, which is what this water sits in and wets
MobLightslocal lights for the mobile forward rendering path, and fog to put them in
MobFortstylized unlit character masters, so the characters standing in this water read the same anywhere
Gradient Tool (required)stylized water is a gradient indexed by depth, and a gradient you cannot edit without recompiling is not a gradient
Non-destructive synced net clockwhat to bind to the time source. Corrected by rate rather than by snapping, so the waves never jump
Forward Render Helperputs your light vector on the MPC. Replaces the hotpatch module in the guide above
This is not Single Layer Water. It does not reflect the sky the way a shading model does, there is no planar reflection behind it, and its ripples are decoration a server cannot see. What it does is work with a frame that has no spare passes, and cost one translucent plane.
