Foam in the shoreline's frame

This is the one preset that turns Foam Texture on, and it is the setting that most repays looking at closely. The pattern is sampled in a frame built from the bank: one axis runs along the shore, the other runs away from it. So the streaks are perpendicular to the water's edge on every side of the body at once.

A foam texture carried by the water's own flow streaks the same way all round a pool, and looks right on exactly one side of it. That is the reason for the separate frame, and it is why the setting is a compiled permutation - the coordinates are their own arithmetic and a body with plain foam should not pay for them.

Remember the cost: setting a per-body Foam Texture asset gives that body a dynamic material instance and takes it out of the batch. Leave it unset and the body uses the material's own texture, which is what the preset expects.

Detail, and where it stops working

Three normal scales are summed: two scrolling detail layers at different tilings, and a slow macro swell. Detail Strength scales the first two, Macro Strength the third.

Detail Scroll Speedhow fast the detail drifts with no flow and no waves. Water with a still surface still moves. 0 gives genuinely still water, which no combination of flow and wave amplitude can produce, because neither drives this
Flow Velocityadvects the detail and the caustics, and biases foam. It does not carry a swimmer
Detail normals tile, and a tiling that is fine enough to read close up is a shimmering mess at distance. Judge it at the range the player actually stands, and remember mip selection under ES3.1 is not the same as under SM5.

What realism cannot have here

No planar reflectionthe sky is a long-latitude texture weighted by Fresnel. It reflects a sky, not a scene: a jetty does not appear upside down under itself
No sky atmosphere termthis renderer has it off, which is part of why the glint carries so much of the lighting
No spray or mistcrest foam says where the water is breaking; a project's own Niagara is what puts anything in the air
No wet shorelinethe bank does not darken where water has been. That is the surface material's job - MobMaterials carries a wetness input for it
