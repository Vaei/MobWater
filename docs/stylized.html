Stylized

Water that reads by shape rather than by depth. Two presets: one that still behaves like water, and one that does not pretend to.

The difference between stylized and realistic water is not one setting, it is a dozen agreeing with each other. A hard shallow colour wants tight foam and no detail normal; a soft absorption wants the opposite. Set half of them and the result reads as neither.

Every value in a look preset is per-body custom primitive data, so the stylized pool and the realistic harbour in the same level are the same material and share every shader permutation. A preset costs nothing to apply and nothing to have.

Stylized

WL_MobWater_Stylized. Bright, saturated, and shallow water that is almost not there - the read is that you can see the bed through it and the colour is in the depth, not on the surface.

SettingWhy
Shallow Color0.20, 0.85, 0.80nearly the deep colour, so clarity does the work rather than the tint
Deep Color0.02, 0.35, 0.70
Fade Depth260short, so the grade happens where you can see it
Clarity Depth900long, so the bed stays visible well out from the bank. This single value is what separates the references from painted glass
Detail Strength0.35high frequency detail is what makes a surface read as photographed
Foam Sharpness8a cut line, not a wet margin
Foam Noise Amount0the contour follows the shoreline exactly. A wandering edge is what reads as texture on a stylized surface, whatever the foam itself is doing
Foam Bands0hard edged, and not banded - see below
Caustic Strength0.55bright and shallow-focused. In the references the caustic is a major read, not a subtlety
Glint Threshold0.18distinct but not stamped. Stylized water sparkles; toon water has marks put on it
Glint Emissive3.0the sparkle blooms past what the sun could account for
Refractionon, 0.45the bed bending is most of why it reads as liquid
Bands are off here on purpose. Quantising foam only works once it is already a narrow band - applied to a region it turns the noise into mottled blotches, which is worse than the gradient it replaced.

Toon

WL_MobWater_Toon. Flat colour, one foam line with a gap cut into it, and no sky at all. Tuned against a real pool rather than derived, so the values are the ones that were looked at.

SettingWhy
Min Opacity0.95the bed is gone. Letting depth decide leaves a shallow pool showing the ground, which reads as the water being textured
Clarity Depth65
Unlit0.5half emitted. All of it flattens the body into a decal; none of it lets a skylight that captured a field turn blue water green
Detail Strength0.0001present but almost nothing. Zero is flatter than the reference and loses the last trace of movement
Reflection Strength0a Fresnel reflection is a smooth ramp across the whole body and reads as varnish
Foam Bands1one band
Foam Band Separation0.75three quarters of it cut away. More bands crowd the shoreline; the separation is what gives the single line its gap
Foam Noise Amount0.4the line wanders, which is the one place texture is wanted
Edge Foam Width0.25wide, because on a body this opaque the shoreline term has nothing to work with
Refractionoffnothing behind the surface is visible to bend
Caustic Strength0.05a trace. The bed is hidden, so this is only what catches at the very edge
Several of these read as compromises on paper and are not. The surface keeps a trace of detail and half its lighting, because fully flat and fully emitted lost the water's shape entirely - it stopped being a body and became a blue polygon.

The gradient fork

MobWaterGradientCoord exists in the shader library and turns the water column into a 0-1 coordinate a gradient ramp can be indexed by, for hard bands in any palette from the same tap. It is written and not wired up: the presets above get their look from absorption, opacity and foam instead. GradientTool is the intended companion for it.

Making your own

  1. Duplicate the closest preset rather than starting from the defaults. The defaults are a neutral pool, not a starting point for a direction.
  2. Set the two colours, Fade Depth and Clarity Depth first, with foam off. Everything else sits on top of that read.
  3. Then foam, then glint. Both are easy to over-set against a colour that is not settled.
  4. Judge it under the ES3.1 preview, and at the distance the player sees it.
