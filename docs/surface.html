Surface

Every property on UMobWaterComponent, in the order the details panel puts them.

All of it is per-body custom primitive data unless a row says otherwise, so nothing here costs a material instance and a level can carry as many looks as it likes for one set of shaders.

Foam

Foam is a compiled variant, so a body without it carries neither the foam samples nor their maths.

PropertyDefault
Foamonshoreline, edge and crest foam
Foam Opacity1.0how much foam there is at all, whatever it is made of. Scales the finished mask after the bands and edges are worked out, so turning it down thins every band evenly instead of eating them from the shore outwards
Shore Foam Depth8 cmhow far up from the bed foam reaches. A line, not a region
Edge Foam Width0.12how far in from the bank the edge line reaches, as a fraction of the shore fade. Every body gets this whether or not anything is under it
Crest Foam Threshold0.55how hard the surface has to fold before it breaks white. 1 is never
Foam Sharpness3.0how hard the edge is. Low is a wet fade up a beach, high is a stylized cut
Foam Noise Amount0how far noise moves the edge, as a fraction of the band width. It moves where the foam ends, not how bright it is
Foam Bands0cuts it into this many steps. 0 leaves it smooth. A wake is left out of the banding, because banding rounds every step up and would turn the faintest trail into a solid slab with a hard edge
Foam Band Separation0how much water shows between the bands. 0 leaves them touching, which reads as one band with steps in it
Foam Textureofffoam carries a pattern of its own, in the shoreline's frame. A compiled variant
Foam Texture (asset)—not per-body data. Setting it takes a dynamic material instance and drops the body out of the batch. Unset uses the material's own
Foam Texture Opacity0with the foam's own texture off, how much of the water underneath shows through. With it on, how strongly the texture marks the foam
Three of these share data slots, because the primitive data is full at thirty six floats: bands with their separation, noise amount with texture opacity, and edge width with foam opacity. Each pair is quantised to a hundredth on the whole-number side. It is invisible in use and it is why those sliders have the ranges they do.
